<objective>
Repair the evidence-integrity defects that the 222-01 audit found in the pre-existing, already-stanced Collin cohort (57 people, 220 rows) — delete the 12 Class A stances that were written without evidence and hand-review the 19 Class B2 weak-adjacency stances, keeping only those that genuinely cite an explicit on-topic statement, vote, or questionnaire answer by the specific person — so no Collin profile shows a chair the sources do not support. This plan writes NO new stances; its only writes are deletions logged to the blank register.

Purpose: at the 222-01 Task 3 blocking checkpoint the operator declined to close this plan as a Plano no-op and instead asked for a county-wide double-check of the early-pass cohort (stanced before this phase's evidence bar was written down). That check found 12 rows that flatly violate D-04 — no evidence at all, party named in reasoning, identity-inferred, or policy-defaulted from a city-wide law rather than the person's own words — and 19 more whose reasoning is adjacency-only rather than an explicit on-topic citation. Both sets must be resolved before Phase 222 can honestly claim the Collin dataset is evidence-only.
Output: one AUDIT-ONLY remediation migration in the EV-Accounts repo, a `## Phase 222 integrity remediation (2026-07-25)` section in `222-CONFIRMED-BLANK.md`, and applied production deletions.
</objective>
